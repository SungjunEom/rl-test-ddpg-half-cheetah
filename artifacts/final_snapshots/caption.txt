Final-policy snapshots of PPO (left) and DDPG (right) in HalfCheetah-v5 after 1,000,000 training transitions. Both use training seed 1, deterministic actions, evaluation reset seed 100000, and the same camera settings. Frames are taken after transition 50 (2.5 s) in each evaluation rollout. Final refers to the training checkpoint, not the last episode frame. The common timestep was selected before rendering. These single-run illustrations show instantaneous posture, not gait, speed, or performance across seeds.
